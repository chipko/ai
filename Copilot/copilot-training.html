Copilot Training Studio

Everything is scripted and offline. No Microsoft 365 account, tenant or real data is used, so it is safe to present anywhere.

Records the stage as a small, PowerPoint-ready MP4 (in recent Chrome or Edge). When asked what to share, choose this tab. The presenter bar and pointer are hidden while recording.

Every scene, prompt, answer, document and caption is in this JSON. Edit it for your audience, then Apply. Download it to share with other trainers; they can Import it or drop the .json on the page.

Presenting

→ or Next runs the next step (types a prompt, then shows Copilot's answer). Press → during typing to finish it instantly. Space toggles auto-play for the scene. ← and Shift+→ move between scenes; 1–9 jump straight to a scene. R restarts, E finishes the scene, T switches light/dark, H hides the presenter bar (hover the bottom edge to bring it back), F is fullscreen, + / − changes text size.
